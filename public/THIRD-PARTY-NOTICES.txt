MUTOPIA CATALOGUE (experimental "Browse Mutopia" page)
  public/songs/mutopia-index.json lists titles, composers, licences and links of
  openly licensed Mutopia Project pieces (https://www.mutopiaproject.org,
  github.com/MutopiaProject/MutopiaProject). Pieces opened from that page are
  downloaded from GitHub and converted in the browser; their licence is shown
  with each piece. They are not bundled with the app.

Hand transcriptions in songs/*.notes (compiled to public/songs/*.musicxml)
carry the licence stated in each file's header.

Private scores placed in public/songs/local/ are not part of this project,
are not committed, and are the responsibility of whoever adds them.
